## Goal

Keep an archive navigable for **future you**, without forcing a complex system on day one.

## Folders

- Prefer **broad folders** first (year, life area, project) and split only when volume demands it.
- If you are unsure, use a single inbox folder and sort weekly.

## Templates

Templates reduce decision fatigue for repeating captures:

- weekly reviews
- trip logs
- meeting notes
- health check-ins

## Naming

Consistency beats cleverness. A boring, repeatable title pattern often outperforms tags you will not remember.
