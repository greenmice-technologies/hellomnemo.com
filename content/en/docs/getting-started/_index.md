Use these pages in order the first time you open Mnemo.

1. [Create an account (optional)](create-account/) — only required for cloud-backed features.
2. [Your first memory](first-memory/) — capture and find a first entry.
3. [Import & export](import-export/) — move data in and out as a portable archive.

When you are comfortable with basics, continue with [Core workflows](../core-workflows/).
