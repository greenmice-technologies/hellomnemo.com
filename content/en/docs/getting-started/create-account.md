Mnemo works **offline-first**. Many people use it locally for a long time before enabling cloud features.

## When an account helps

Create a HelloMnemo account when you want:

- **Encrypted sync** of your dataset and media across devices
- **Continuity** when you replace a phone or use multiple devices
- Access to **cloud-backed** capabilities offered in your build (availability varies by release and platform)

## What to expect

- Sign-in flows are implemented in the app using standard provider-based authentication where applicable.
- Your archive remains **yours**; cloud features are optional and can be ignored if you prefer a purely local workflow.

## Privacy note

For website visitor privacy (not the Mnemo app), see [Privacy & cookies](../../privacy-cookies/). For how Mnemo handles your data in the app, see [Privacy & security](../privacy-and-security/).
