Mnemo supports **import and export** workflows so your memory stays portable.

## Why it matters

- **Backup**: keep a copy independent of any single device.
- **Migration**: move to a new phone or tablet with less friction.
- **Peace of mind**: your archive is not “trapped” in one install.

## Practical guidance

Exact UI labels change over time, but the intent is stable:

1. Use **export** to produce an archive package that contains your dataset and referenced media.
2. Store that package somewhere you control (encrypted disk, personal cloud storage, etc.).
3. Use **import** on a new install when you want to restore or merge carefully (be mindful of duplicates).

## Safety tips

- Treat exports like sensitive data—**encrypt storage** and limit sharing.
- After major changes, make a fresh export rather than assuming an old backup is current.
