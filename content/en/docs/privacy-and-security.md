## Local-first

Assume your device holds the authoritative copy of your archive unless you explicitly enable sync.

## Account-backed features

If you use a HelloMnemo account, encrypted sync may upload ciphertext dataset and media blobs to HelloMnemo infrastructure. The intent is that your content is not handled as plaintext for everyday operations.

## Hygiene checklist

- keep your OS updated
- use a strong device passcode
- enable disk encryption where available
- keep exports in protected storage
