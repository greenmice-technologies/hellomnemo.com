Start narrow, measure, then escalate.

## I cannot sign in

- confirm network connectivity
- confirm system date/time is correct
- retry after updating the app

## Sync looks stuck

- verify free storage on the device
- confirm you are signed into the same account on each device
- leave the app open on a stable network for a full sync cycle

## Missing media

- check whether the entry references media that was never fully downloaded
- confirm the file type is supported on your platform

## Import/export problems

- verify you are using a current export format from the same major app generation
- keep a second copy of exports before attempting destructive restores

## Still blocked

Email [info@hellomnemo.com](mailto:info@hellomnemo.com) with:

- platform and app version
- what you expected vs what happened
- whether the issue involves sync, media, or import/export
