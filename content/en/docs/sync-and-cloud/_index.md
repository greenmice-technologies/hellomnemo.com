Mnemo’s cloud features are **optional**. Local use remains a first-class path.

## What sync is for

- keep an encrypted copy consistent across devices
- recover faster when you replace hardware
- enable cloud-backed capabilities offered in your build

## What sync is not

- a public sharing platform
- a replacement for your own backups if you require strong disaster recovery guarantees

## Operational expectations

Network, provider sign-in, and device storage all affect sync reliability. If something looks stuck, start with [Troubleshooting](../troubleshooting/).
