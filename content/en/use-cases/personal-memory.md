## Outcome

A calm, trustworthy place where everyday moments and important chapters stay **findable**—without turning journaling into a chore.

## What people capture

- Travel notes with photos and places
- Health and habit logs (as private as you want them)
- Family milestones and kid quotes you will want later
- “Slow” reflections you revisit across months

## What makes it work in Mnemo

- **Offline-first**: capture even when connectivity is bad.
- **Context by default**: dates, locations, and media stay attached to the entry.
- **Recall surfaces**: calendar and map help you browse time and place—not just keywords.

## Suggested starting structure

- One folder per year, or per “season” of life
- Lightweight templates for recurring check-ins (weekly review, trip log)
- A single “Inbox” folder if you prefer to sort later
