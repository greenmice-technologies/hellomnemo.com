## Outcome

A personal system for **thinking in public… privately**: an archive you can search when the next decision shows up, without losing the “why” behind yesterday’s conclusion.

## What people capture

- Meeting notes with decisions, risks, and follow-ups
- Research snippets and references tied to projects
- Draft thinking that matures over multiple sessions
- Personal operating procedures and checklists

## What makes it work in Mnemo

- **Fast capture** reduces friction at the moment ideas appear
- **Stable organization** prevents “note graveyards”
- **Search-first recall** helps you pull the right note during the next meeting

## Suggested starting structure

- A folder per client, product area, or initiative
- A template for meetings (agenda, decisions, actions)
- A “Reading inbox” for material you process weekly
