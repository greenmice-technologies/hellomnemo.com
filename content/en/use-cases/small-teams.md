## Outcome

Small groups stay aligned on **what was decided and why**, while individuals keep ownership of their own memory practice.

## Typical patterns

- Shared conventions (templates, naming) instead of heavy tooling
- Individuals keep private archives; export or summaries when collaboration needs a handoff
- A “team log” habit for milestones, incidents, and retrospectives

## Boundaries

Mnemo is **personal-memory first**. If your organization needs enterprise-wide permissions, audit trails, and mandatory retention policies, you will likely combine Mnemo with dedicated collaboration systems.

## Suggested starting structure

- A shared template pack (retro, incident note, decision log)
- A cadence: weekly highlights captured in a consistent format
- Clear ownership: who curates folders vs who only captures
