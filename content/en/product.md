Mnemo helps you keep a **private, durable archive** of life and work fragments—without turning memory into performance.

### What Mnemo is

- **A personal Memory OS**: capture with context, organize with folders and templates, and resurface what matters when it helps.
- **Offline-first**: your primary dataset stays on device so you can write and browse without depending on the network.
- **Optional cloud continuity**: when you choose to use a HelloMnemo account, Mnemo can sync your **encrypted dataset and media** across devices using the platform backend.

### The core loop

1. **Capture** — notes, photos, video, audio, documents, and reminders in one calm workflow.
2. **Preserve structure** — folders and templates that match how you think, not a rigid schema.
3. **Recall** — search, calendar, map, and notifications that bring the right fragment back at the right time.

### Who it is for

- People who want a **long-horizon archive** (years, not days) with real privacy defaults.
- Knowledge workers who need **context-rich notes** they can find months later.
- Small teams that want **shared clarity** without building a corporate knowledge base overnight.

### Practical boundaries

Mnemo is not a social network, a public publishing platform, or a generic team wiki. It is built for **personal memory first**, with export paths when you need to move or back up your data.
